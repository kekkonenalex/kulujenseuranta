Otso - Expenses · design directions

Four looks for the expense app. Pick one.

All four keep what makes it Otso's: true black, the bear, and Otso's two typefaces, plus a mono face in one look. Each has its own colour and its own logo, so it reads as a sibling app, not another tab of the Otso app. Over budget is red in every look, as falling is in Otso Markets.

Each look shows the same two screens: logging an expense, and the month. The numbers are examples, not your data.

A

Kulta · amber rays

Otso's aurora, recoloured for money: amber into copper. The bear is drawn in rays, as on Otso's mark. The month is drawn the same way: one ray a day, as tall as that day's spending.

Signature: the month as rays, one per day. The aurora becomes the chart.

B

Kuitti · the receipt

Every screen is a till receipt on black: paper white, a mono face for the figures, dotted lines out to the amounts, a torn bottom edge. Otso's green-to-teal survives as the strip the printer starts with. The logo is a receipt with the bear's ears.

Signature: the torn edge. Every month ends like a receipt, with a total.

C

Kynsi · the claw tally

The closest sibling: Otso's own green into teal, rays in teal. The logo is four claw marks and a slash, a tally of five: the bear counting. Budgets are drawn as tally marks too, one mark for every 10 €, so you can count what's left.

D

Raanu · the woven stripe

From the Finnish woven blanket: birch white, ochre, rust and slate in bands of different widths. The bear is cut from the weave. The month is one woven bar, each band a category as wide as its share, so the whole month reads in one line. Warm and handmade, the furthest from a tech app.
